## Description

---

Tired of using Latin "Lorem Ipsum" for designs that need to show Indian languages?

Desi Ipsum fills your text layers with realistic placeholder text in 15 Indian languages, with the right script, a font made for it, and native punctuation. Need the classics instead? Lorem ipsum and Shakespeare are one click away.

Works in Figma Design, FigJam, Figma Slides and Figma Buzz.


What's new

New languages: Nepali, Assamese, Santali (Ol Chiki) and Manipuri (Meitei Mayek)
Fill text box: fill each layer exactly to its size: fixed boxes, auto height, or a single auto-width line
Quick actions: type "Desi Ipsum" in Figma's quick actions, pick a language and an amount, done. No window needed
Regenerate: filled layers get a "Regenerate text" button in the right panel for fresh text with the same settings
Western classics: Lorem ipsum and Shakespeare (English placeholder text from his public-domain works)
Light and dark: follows your Figma theme, or pick one yourself
Fill whole frames: select a frame, card or section to fill every text layer inside it
Nothing selected? A new text layer is created in the middle of your view
Keeps your style: Bold stays Bold, layers you've named keep their names, and your last amount is remembered


Features

15 Indian languages: Assamese, Bangla, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Nepali, Odia, Punjabi, Sanskrit, Santali, Tamil and Telugu
Words, sentences, paragraphs, or fill the text box
A suitable font for each script, applied automatically
Native punctuation: । and ॥ in Devanagari and Bangla, ᱾ in Ol Chiki, ꯫ in Meitei Mayek
Select many text layers and fill them all at once, each with different text
Works across Figma Design, FigJam, Slides and Buzz


How to use

1. Select one or more text layers, or a whole frame (or nothing, to create a new text layer)
2. Choose how much text, or "Fill text box"
3. Click a language

Faster: open quick actions (Ctrl/⌘ + /), type "Desi Ipsum", then pick a language and an amount like "3 sentences", "10 words" or "fill".


What is "Desi"?

"Desi" is a word derived from Sanskrit "Desa/Desam" (देश/देशम्), which means country or nation. It is commonly used to describe the people, culture, languages, and products of Indian origin.


Important note

The generated text is randomly composed and does not convey any literal meaning. It is designed purely as visual placeholder text, to help you design and prototype interfaces with authentic-looking Indian language text.
